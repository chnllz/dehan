China-Europe rail is the trunk line. Ocean, road, air and multimodal fill the gaps at either end, so a shipment can be quoted, booked and tracked as one job rather than four.

FCL or LCL

Which one fits your volume

The rule of thumb is simple: past about 15 cubic metres, FCL is usually cheaper and always simpler. Below that, LCL avoids paying for air you do not use.

FCL, full container load

Your cargo occupies the container, the seal is yours, and nothing is co-loaded. Best for steady volumes, high-value goods and anything that must not be handled twice. We can quote 40HQ, 40GP and 20GP, and out-of-gauge where the terminal allows it.

LCL, less than container load

You pay for the space you use. Cargo is consolidated with other shipments at our warehouse, which suits trial orders, samples and small replenishments. Transit is a few days longer because of the consolidation and deconsolidation at each end.

How it works

Six steps from enquiry to proof of delivery

Every step has a named contact. You get routing and pricing first, then a booking confirmation, then tracking until the POD is issued.

1

Enquiry

Send commodity, weight, volume, origin and destination. We come back with routing and pricing within 24 hours.

2

Booking

Once confirmed, we reserve space on the next scheduled departure from the nearest contracted terminal.

3

Collection

Truck collection anywhere in China, or delivery into our warehouse at the port or inland station.

4

Border

Declaration and border crossing at Alashankou, Khorgos, Erenhot or Manzhouli, with gauge change, typically about one day.

5

Line haul

The block train runs to the European hub, with GPS and BeiDou position and electronic seal status throughout.

6

Delivery

Customs clearance at destination, then last-mile delivery to your door or distribution centre, with POD issued.
