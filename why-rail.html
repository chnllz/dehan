RAIL VS SEA VS AIR

Rail is the middle path that usually wins

Roughly two-thirds faster than ocean, about one-fifth the cost of air, and unaffected by port congestion or air traffic control. Services run year-round in both directions.

When rail wins

Restocking cycles of three to five weeks, cargo too valuable to sit on water and too bulky to fly. Also anything exposed to peak-season port delays.

When ocean still wins

Heavy, low-value cargo with no deadline, and shipments where the deepest possible saving matters more than six weeks of inventory.

When air is worth it

Launch stock, spare parts and anything where a missed date costs more than the freight. We quote all three, so the comparison is honest.

Green logistics

Rail moves your cargo with far less carbon

Rail is the lowest-carbon way to move freight across Eurasia. If your customers ask about emissions in tenders, or you report Scope 3 figures, we can give you the numbers for each shipment.

  • Rail produces roughly one-fifteenth of the CO2 of air freight per tonne-kilometre, and materially less than road.
  • One through wagon from origin to destination avoids repeated handling and reloading, which cuts emissions and damage risk together.
  • The trunk lines are largely electrified, so emissions fall further as the grid decarbonises.
  • Rail avoids port and airway congestion, removing the extra emissions caused by waiting and rerouting.

Emissions data you can actually use
