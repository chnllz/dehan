ABOUT US

DH EURASIA RAILLINK LIMITED

A China-Europe rail forwarder built on two hubs rather than one: Shenzhen at the coast, Xi'an inland. Founded in 2026, with contracted capacity at nine Chinese rail terminals.

Why two hubs, not one

Most forwarders are either coastal or inland. Ours sits at both ends of the same problem: cargo arrives in China by sea at Shenzhen, and it leaves for Europe by rail from the interior. Putting an office at each end means the handover between the two is our job, not yours.

That combination is also why we can quote a single through rate from a sea port to a European door, on one contract and one set of documents, instead of stitching two providers together.

SHENZHEN · COASTAL GATEWAY

Our registered office is in Luohu, Shenzhen. Ocean imports, export consolidation and the Pinghu South rail terminal are all in the same city, so sea cargo can be transferred to rail without a cross-country move first.

XI'AN · INLAND RAIL HUB

Our second office sits at the origin of the Xi'an express. This is where the block train to Malaszewicze, Hamburg, Duisburg, Prague, Milan and Budapest departs, and where our transit commitments are set.
