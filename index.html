Why rail

Faster than sea, cheaper than air

Rail sits between the two modes most buyers already use, and on most China-Europe lanes it is the one that balances cost against transit.

Two-thirds faster than ocean

12 to 22 days to our European hubs, against 35 to 45 days by sea, and the schedule does not move with port congestion.

About one-fifth the cost of air

Air gets you there in 3 to 7 days at several times the price. Rail takes the middle ground where most cargo actually belongs.

Year-round, fixed departures

Weekly block trains in both directions, with border crossing and gauge change at Alashankou, Khorgos, Erenhot or Manzhouli.
